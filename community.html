Parents & Family

Parents & Guardians (PTA)

Active parent involvement is one of the pillars of excellence at Francis Maria Libermann School. Our Parent-Teacher Association meets regularly to discuss student welfare, academic progress, and campus enhancements.

How to Get Involved:
  • • Attend scheduled term parent-teacher meetings.
  • • Volunteer for campus events, career talks, and celebrations.
  • • Join our WhatsApp Parent Group for fast announcements.

Graduates & Mentors

Alumni Network

Since 1994, our graduates have achieved great success in university, public service, entrepreneurship, and community leadership across Tanzania and the world.

Alumni Mentorship Program:
  • • Share your university & career journeys with Form 3 & 4 candidates.
  • • Provide internship and professional coaching to young alumni.
  • • Participate in the annual FMLS Alumni Homecoming reunion.
