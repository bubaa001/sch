School Life at Francis Maria Libermann School

Experience a vibrant and nurturing environment where students grow with character, faith, and purpose.

A Day in the Life

At Francis Maria Libermann School, school life is a blend of academic rigor, spiritual growth, and community engagement, guided by our motto, "Education Key to Liberty".

Boarding Environment

Our boarding school environment fosters discipline, camaraderie, and personal growth. Students start their day with morning prayers in the chapel, followed by a structured timetable of classes, healthy meals, study periods, and extracurricular activities.

Evenings are dedicated to supervised prep in the classrooms and dormitories, ensuring focused academic reinforcement, while weekends offer opportunities for sports, cultural events, and community service.

Holistic Character Formation

We emphasize holistic development, encouraging students to participate in debate clubs, music, sports, and spiritual retreats. Our family spirit ensures that every student feels supported, valued, and empowered to navigate the challenges of adolescence and adulthood.

Mentorship programs pairing senior and junior students create lifelong bonds of brotherhood, responsibility, and mutual respect.
