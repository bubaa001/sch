Admissions & Registration

Begin your journey at Francis Maria Libermann School. Learn how to apply and secure your enrollment.

Application & Registration Instructions

Admissions applications and registration forms are processed directly through our campus Admissions Office.

How to Apply:
  1. Pick Up Registration Form: Visit our campus office in Shehia of Tomondo, Zanzibar, or request an application package via WhatsApp or email.
  2. Prepare Required Documents:
    • Certified Copy of Birth Certificate
    • Recent Report Cards (last two academic years)
    • Medical Report & Vaccination records
    • Parent/Guardian Identification
    • Transfer Certificate (if transferring from another school)
  3. Registration Fee: A non-refundable registration fee of TZS 50,000 is required (payable directly to the school's NMB account).
  4. Entrance Assessment & Interview: All applicants complete an entrance assessment and family interview with our Admissions Team.
  5. Enrollment: Successful candidates receive their acceptance letter and fee schedule.
Admissions Office Hours: Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM | Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM.

Conditions for Enrollment

We welcome students who are ready to embrace our values-driven education and contribute to our community.

  • Age Requirement: Applicants for Nursery must be at least 3 years old by September 30 of the admission year. For higher classes, age is assessed based on the student's previous academic level.
  • Academic Performance: Applicants must demonstrate satisfactory performance in their previous school, as evidenced by report cards, and pass our entrance exam.
  • Entrance Exam and Interview: All applicants are required to take an entrance exam covering English, Mathematics, and General Knowledge, followed by an interview with the Admissions Team.
  • Character and Discipline: Applicants must exhibit good behavior and a willingness to adhere to the school's Catholic values and code of conduct, though students of all faiths are welcome.
  • Boarding Requirement: Students willing to board are encouraged to apply, as Francis Maria Libermann School operates as both a day and boarding school, fostering a disciplined and supportive environment.
  • Health Requirements: A medical report confirming the student's fitness and up-to-date vaccinations is mandatory.

Our Academic Programs

Preparatory & Non-Candidate Classes

We offer a Pre-Form 1 Preparatory Program for students who are not yet ready to join Form 1 but show potential for growth. This program focuses on foundational skills in English, Mathematics, and Science, preparing students for the rigors of secondary education.

  • Intensive foundational academic support
  • Character development workshops
  • Introduction to boarding life and routine

Candidate Classes (NECTA Curriculum)

Francis Maria Libermann School offers education from Nursery to Form 4, preparing students for national examinations (PSLE, FTNA, CSEE) under the NECTA curriculum (school codes ZP0372 & S4202).

  • Academic excellence in core science and arts subjects
  • Practical learning through modern science laboratories
  • Spiritual and moral growth through our Catholic ethos
  • Extracurricular activities, including sports and cultural events
