School Fee Structure

Clear and transparent fee information for all educational levels at Francis Maria Libermann School

Registration Fees

One-time registration fees payable before students' admission

Student Category Registration Fee (TZS)
Kindergarten registration fee 50,000
Kindergarten students continuing to standard one from FMLS 55,000
Students transferring from other schools to join primary 80,000
Students transferring from other schools to join secondary 120,000
FMLS primary students continuing secondary studies FREE
Note: Registration fee is non-refundable and must be paid before the student is accepted into the school.

School Parent Meetings

School and parent meetings are very important for communication between teachers and parents. It helps track academic progress, assignments, and school activities throughout the year.

Students and parents will be informed by the administration on the exact day meetings are scheduled.

Note: School meetings must be attended by parents or guardians when the date is announced.

Payment Information & Bank Details

Official Payment Guidelines

Important Payment Rules:
  • School fees once paid are non-refundable. Please verify details before paying.
  • All Term payments must be made in full before school opens in January and July.
  • All payments must be made through the bank using the student's full name as reference.
  • After payment, present the bank deposit slip to the school accounts office for an official receipt.
